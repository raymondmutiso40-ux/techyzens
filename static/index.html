Slide 8: Safety & Human Control Architecture

"Safe by design, not by promise." Built so that nothing leaves your hands without a person saying yes.

01 No Auto-Submit

The system stops at a draft. Submitting is always a person's call.

02 Spend Caps & Tripwires

Daily cap: $50.00 | Per-tender cap: $5.00. Automatic cutoff and alerts before they bite.

03 Untrusted Documents

Tender files are sanitized and read strictly as isolated data, never as system instructions.

04 Grounded Claims

Every claim in a draft points to its verifiable source. Unsupported claims are flagged in amber.
